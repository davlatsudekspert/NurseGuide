Qisqacha

NurseGuide — o‘quv va ma’lumotnoma ilovasi, rasmiy buyruqlar va klinik mulohazaning o‘rnini bosmaydi. Har bir ma’lumot ostida manbasi ko‘rsatilgan. Ilova bepul, reklamasiz.

1. Umumiy qoidalar

NurseGuide — hamshiralar, hamshiralik ta’limi talabalari va ustozlari uchun bepul o‘quv va ma’lumotnoma ilovasi. Ilovadan foydalanib yoki hisob ochib, siz ushbu shartlarga va maxfiylik siyosatiga rozilik bildirasiz. Rozi bo‘lmasangiz, ilovadan foydalanmang.

2. Tibbiy cheklov

NurseGuide o‘quv va ma’lumotnoma ilovasi — tibbiy xizmat ham, tibbiy qurilma ham emas. U tashxis qo‘ymaydi, davolashni yoki dori dozasini buyurmaydi va bemorga yordam ko‘rsatish bo‘yicha qaror qabul qilish uchun mo‘ljallanmagan.

Ilovadagi ma’lumotlar rasmiy buyruqlar, klinik protokollar, muassasangizning ichki tartiblari, dori vositalarining rasmiy yo‘riqnomalari va shifokor hamda hamshiralik mutaxassisining klinik fikrini almashtirmaydi. Bemorga tegishli har qanday qarorda amaldagi rasmiy hujjatlarga, rahbaringiz ko‘rsatmalariga va o‘z klinik mulohazangizga tayaning. Shoshilinch holatda tez yordam xizmatiga murojaat qiling.

3. Manbalar va aniqlik

Ilovadagi har bir o‘quv ma’lumoti va test savolining ostida uning manbasi ko‘rsatiladi. Biz materiallarni tekshirib, yangilab boramiz, lekin ularning to‘liqligi, dolzarbligi va xatosizligiga kafolat bermaymiz: tibbiy me’yorlar o‘zgaradi. Manba va ilova o‘rtasida ziddiyat bo‘lsa, asl manba va amaldagi rasmiy hujjat ustun. Xato topsangiz, “Taklif va yordam” orqali yoki emailga xabar bering.

Test natijalari va ballar faqat o‘z bilimingizni baholash uchun; ular imtihon yoki attestatsiyadagi natijani kafolatlamaydi.

4. Hisob

Mehmon sifatida bosh sahifa, manipulyatsiyalar ro‘yxati, ish joyi va kutubxonani ko‘rish mumkin; testlar, mashq, kunlik savollar, guruhlar va murojaatlar uchun bepul hisob kerak. Kirish emailga yuborilgan bir martalik kod orqali (yoki ixtiyoriy ravishda Google/Apple orqali) amalga oshiriladi. Hisob xavfsizligi va u orqali qilingan harakatlar uchun o‘zingiz javobgarsiz. Faqat o‘zingizga tegishli email manzildan foydalaning. Hisobni istalgan vaqtda ilovada o‘chirishingiz mumkin.

5. Ustoz va talaba guruhlari

Talaba guruhga qo‘shilganda ustoz uning taxallusini (yoki tartib raqamini) va guruh testlaridagi natijalarini ko‘radi. Ustoz guruhni faqat o‘quv maqsadida boshqaradi va talabalarning natijalarini boshqalarga oshkor qilmaydi. Ustozlik huquqi ilovadagi rol tanloviga emas, server tasdig‘iga bog‘liq.

6. Foydalanish qoidalari

Quyidagilar taqiqlanadi: xizmatga, serverga yoki boshqa foydalanuvchilarning hisoblariga ruxsatsiz kirish yoki ularni buzishga urinish; avtomatlashtirilgan so‘rovlar bilan xizmatga ortiqcha yuk tushirish; test kalitlarini yoki boshqalar natijalarini aldov yo‘li bilan olish; haqoratli, noqonuniy yoki boshqa shaxsning shaxsiy ma’lumotini o‘z ichiga olgan matnni murojaat va taxallusda yozish; ilovani tijorat maqsadida qayta sotish yoki tarqatish. Qoidabuzar hisob cheklanishi yoki o‘chirilishi mumkin.

7. Mualliflik huquqi

Ilovaning matnlari, savollari, dizayni va dasturiy kodi NurseGuide mualliflariga tegishli, tashqi manbalar esa o‘z litsenziyalari bilan ko‘rsatilgan. Ularni shaxsiy o‘qish uchun ishlatishingiz mumkin; tijorat maqsadida nusxalash va tarqatish mumkin emas.

8. Xizmat mavjudligi

Ilova “boricha” taqdim etiladi. Uzilishlar, xatolar va o‘zgarishlar bo‘lishi mumkin; biz ularni imkon qadar tez tuzatamiz, lekin uzluksiz ishlashga kafolat bermaymiz. Ilovaning ayrim imkoniyatlarini oldindan xabar bermay o‘zgartirishimiz yoki to‘xtatishimiz mumkin.

9. Javobgarlikni cheklash

Qonun ruxsat etgan darajada NurseGuide mualliflari ilovadan foydalanish yoki foydalana olmaslik natijasidagi zararlar, shu jumladan ilovadagi ma’lumotga tayanib qabul qilingan klinik, kasbiy yoki o‘quv qarorlari oqibatlari uchun javobgar emas. Bu cheklov qonun bilan chetlab o‘tib bo‘lmaydigan javobgarlikka tegmaydi.

10. To‘xtatish va o‘chirish

Siz hisobni istalgan vaqtda ilovada (Profil → Maxfiylik va ma’lumotlar → Hisobni o‘chirish) yoki nurseguide@nfcstore.uz orqali o‘chirishingiz mumkin: batafsil. Biz shartlar buzilganda hisobni cheklashimiz yoki o‘chirishimiz mumkin.

11. Shartlarning o‘zgarishi

Shartlar o‘zgarsa, ushbu sahifadagi sana yangilanadi va jiddiy o‘zgarishlar haqida ilovada xabar beramiz. O‘zgarishdan keyin ilovadan foydalanishda davom etish yangi shartlarga rozilikni bildiradi.

12. Amaldagi qonun

Ushbu shartlarga O‘zbekiston Respublikasi qonunlari qo‘llaniladi.

13. Aloqa

Savol va takliflar: nurseguide@nfcstore.uz.

NurseGuide is an educational and reference app; it does not replace official orders or clinical judgement. Every item shows its source. The app is free and has no ads.

1. General

NurseGuide is a free study and reference app for nurses, nursing students and nursing teachers. By using the app or creating an account you agree to these terms and to the privacy policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Medical disclaimer

NurseGuide is an educational and reference app — it is not a medical service and not a medical device. It does not diagnose, prescribe treatment or medicine doses, and is not intended for deciding how to care for a patient.

The information in the app does not replace official orders, clinical protocols, your institution’s procedures, official medicine instructions, or the clinical judgement of a physician and a nursing professional. For any patient-related decision rely on the current official documents, your supervisor’s instructions and your own clinical judgement. In an emergency, call the emergency medical service.

3. Sources and accuracy

Every study item and test question in the app shows its source. We review and update the material, but we do not guarantee that it is complete, current or error-free: medical norms change. If the source and the app disagree, the original source and the current official document prevail. If you find a mistake, tell us through “Suggestions and help” or by e-mail.

Test results and scores are only for self-assessment of your knowledge and do not guarantee an outcome in an exam or certification.

4. Account

Guests can browse the home screen, the procedures list, the workplace and the library; tests, practice, daily questions, groups and requests need a free account. Use only an e-mail address that belongs to you. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

5. Teacher and student groups

When a student joins a group, the teacher sees their nickname (or seat number) and the results in the group’s tests. A teacher uses a group for educational purposes only and does not disclose students’ results to others. Teacher rights depend on server confirmation, not on the role chosen in the app.
